# Substance Use and Mental Health in College

Substance use and mental health are deeply connected. Alcohol and drugs are often used to manage emotional distress — and reliably make it worse over time.

## The Relationship Between Substances and Mental Health

- **Short term:** Alcohol and cannabis reduce anxiety acutely, which is why they feel helpful
- **Medium term:** Regular use disrupts sleep architecture, worsens anxiety baseline, and impairs the brain's natural stress-regulation systems
- **Long term:** Heavy or dependent use is a significant risk factor for depression, anxiety disorders, and academic failure

## Warning Signs of Problematic Use

- Using substances primarily to manage emotions (anxiety, loneliness, sleep)
- Use has increased over the academic year
- Difficulty cutting back despite wanting to
- Missing class, assignments, or commitments due to use
- Friends or family expressing concern

## You Don't Have to Quit to Get Support

CAPS counselors can help you understand your relationship with substances without judgment and without requiring abstinence. Harm reduction approaches are evidence-based and available.

## Confidentiality

Discussing substance use with a CAPS counselor is confidential (the same limits apply as any counseling: imminent harm, abuse reporting). Your academic record is not affected.
