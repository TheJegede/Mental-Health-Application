# Mindfulness and Stress Reduction

Mindfulness involves paying attention to the present moment without judgment. Research supports its effectiveness for reducing anxiety, improving mood, and managing stress.

## Free UCLA Resources

### UCLA Mindful App
Free mindfulness meditations from the UCLA Mindful Awareness Research Center (MARC):
- Download: [uclahealth.org/marc/free-guided-meditations](https://www.uclahealth.org/departments/marc/free-guided-meditations)
- Guided meditations: 3–19 minutes, several languages
- Sleep meditations included

### MARC Guided Meditations (Online)
Free audio and video guided practices available at [marc.ucla.edu](https://www.marc.ucla.edu/) — no account required.

### Mindfulness Meditation Classes
UCLA MARC offers free weekly meditation classes open to students, staff, and the public. Check [marc.ucla.edu](https://www.marc.ucla.edu/) for the current schedule.

## Basic Practices to Try Now

**Box breathing (for acute anxiety):**
1. Inhale for 4 counts
2. Hold for 4 counts
3. Exhale for 4 counts
4. Hold for 4 counts
5. Repeat 3–4 times

**5-4-3-2-1 grounding (for overwhelm):**
Name 5 things you can see, 4 you can feel, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell, 1 you can taste.

**Body scan (before sleep):**
Starting from the top of your head, slowly notice and release tension in each part of your body.

## Limits of Self-Help

Self-help tools are useful supplements, not replacements for professional support. If stress is persistent or significantly affecting your functioning, contact CAPS at (310) 825-0768.
