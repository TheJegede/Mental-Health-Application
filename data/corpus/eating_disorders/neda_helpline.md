# National Alliance for Eating Disorders Helpline

Free helpline offering support, resources, and treatment referrals for individuals with eating disorders and their families.

## How to Reach

- **Call 1-866-662-1235**
- Monday–Friday, 9am–7pm ET
- Leave a message after hours; calls returned next business day

## What This Service Provides

- Emotional support and guidance for individuals struggling with eating disorders
- Referrals to treatment providers, support groups, and therapists
- Resources for family members and friends who want to help a loved one

## Eating Disorders Covered

- Anorexia Nervosa
- Bulimia Nervosa
- Binge Eating Disorder
- ARFID (Avoidant/Restrictive Food Intake Disorder)
- Other Specified Feeding and Eating Disorders (OSFED)

## For Immediate Crisis

Eating disorders have the highest mortality rate of any mental health condition. If you or someone you know is in medical danger, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

For emotional crisis support: Call or text **988** or text **NEDA to 741741**.
