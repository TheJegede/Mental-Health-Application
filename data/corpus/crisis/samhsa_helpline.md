# SAMHSA National Helpline

The Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration (SAMHSA) National Helpline is a free, confidential treatment referral and information service.

## How to Reach

- **Call 1-800-662-4357** (1-800-662-HELP)
- Available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year
- English and Spanish

## What This Service Provides

- Information about mental health and substance use conditions
- Referrals to local treatment facilities, support groups, and community-based organizations
- Does not provide counseling; refers callers to appropriate local resources

## Who Can Call

Anyone with questions about mental health, substance use, or both — including family members and friends concerned about a loved one.

## Online Treatment Locator

Find local treatment options at [findtreatment.gov](https://findtreatment.gov/)

## Confidentiality

The service is confidential and free. No insurance required.
