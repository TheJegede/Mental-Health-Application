# 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

If you or someone you know is in a mental health crisis, suicidal, or in emotional distress, the 988 Lifeline provides free, confidential support 24/7.

## How to Reach 988

- **Call or text 988** — available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
- **Chat:** [988lifeline.org/chat](https://988lifeline.org/chat)
- **TTY:** Use your preferred relay service or dial 711, then 988

## Who Answers

Calls and texts are routed to a network of local crisis centers staffed by trained counselors. You will speak with a real person, not an automated system.

## What to Expect

- You can call about yourself or someone you are concerned about
- Counselors listen without judgment and help connect you to local resources
- Calls are confidential; counselors will only involve emergency services if they believe your life or someone else's is in immediate danger

## For Veterans

Press 1 after dialing 988 to reach the Veterans Crisis Line, which is staffed by counselors with military background.

## For LGBTQ+ Youth

Press 3 after dialing 988 to reach the Trevor Project's crisis line.

## If You Are in Immediate Danger

Call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room.
