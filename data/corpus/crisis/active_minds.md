# Active Minds

Active Minds is the leading nonprofit organization supporting mental health awareness and education for young adults, with chapters on more than 1,000 college campuses.

## What Active Minds Does

- Runs student-led chapters that open conversations about mental health on campus
- Provides education and resources to reduce stigma
- Advocates for stronger campus mental health policies

## Resources for Students

- **Find a chapter:** [activeminds.org/programs/active-minds-chapters/](https://www.activeminds.org/programs/active-minds-chapters/)
- **Send Silence Packing:** Traveling exhibit that raises awareness about student suicide
- **Peer support training:** Students can learn to support peers in distress

## Starting a Chapter

If your campus doesn't have an Active Minds chapter, you can start one. Information at [activeminds.org/start-a-chapter](https://www.activeminds.org/start-a-chapter/).
