# Crisis Text Line

Free, 24/7 text-based crisis support. No call required — text from wherever you are.

## How to Reach

- **Text HOME to 741741** from anywhere in the US
- Available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
- Free of charge, works on any phone with SMS

## What Happens When You Text

1. An automated message confirms the connection
2. You are connected with a trained Crisis Counselor within minutes
3. The counselor listens, helps you move from a hot moment to a cool calm, and works with you to create a safety plan

## Who Can Use It

Anyone in the US experiencing a mental health crisis — including anxiety, depression, suicidal thoughts, eating disorders, self-harm, or any overwhelming situation.

## Confidentiality

Conversations are confidential. The counselor will only contact emergency services if there is imminent risk to your life or someone else's.

## If You Are in Immediate Danger

Call 911 or text 911 if your area supports it, or go to your nearest emergency room.
