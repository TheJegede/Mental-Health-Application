# JED Foundation

The JED Foundation is a nonprofit that protects emotional health and prevents suicide among teens and young adults, with a specific focus on college students.

## Resources for Students

- **ULifeline:** Online mental health resource center for college students — [ulifeline.org](https://www.ulifeline.org/)
- **Half of Us:** Campaign addressing mental health stigma on campus — [halfofus.com](https://www.halfofus.com/)
- **SeizeTheAwkward.org:** Guides on how to talk to friends who may be struggling

## If You Are in Crisis Now

- Call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) — 24/7
- Text **HOME to 741741** (Crisis Text Line) — 24/7

## JED Campus Program

JED works directly with colleges to evaluate and strengthen their mental health programs. If you want your campus to implement better mental health systems, direct your institution to [jedfoundation.org/jed-campus](https://jedfoundation.org/jed-campus/).

## Mental Health Warning Signs

Signs that you or a friend may need support:
- Withdrawing from friends, family, and activities
- Changes in eating or sleeping habits
- Talking about hopelessness, being a burden, or wanting to die
- Increased use of alcohol or drugs
- Giving away valued possessions
