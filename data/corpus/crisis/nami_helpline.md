# NAMI HelpLine

The National Alliance on Mental Illness (NAMI) HelpLine is a free peer support service for anyone affected by mental health conditions.

## How to Reach

- **Call 1-800-950-6264** (1-800-950-NAMI)
- **Text "HELPLINE" to 62640**
- **Chat:** [nami.org/help](https://www.nami.org/help)
- Available Monday–Friday, 10am–10pm ET

## What This Service Provides

- Information about mental health conditions and treatment options
- Guidance on navigating the mental health system
- Referrals to local NAMI affiliates and support groups
- Peer support from trained volunteers with lived experience

## Who It Is For

Students, family members, caregivers, and anyone seeking information or support related to mental health.

## Note on Hours

NAMI HelpLine is not a 24/7 crisis line. For after-hours crises, use 988 or Crisis Text Line (text HOME to 741741).
