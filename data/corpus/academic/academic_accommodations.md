# Academic Accommodations for Mental Health at UCLA

If a mental health condition is affecting your academic performance, you may be eligible for accommodations through the Center for Accessible Education (CAE).

## What Accommodations Are Available?

Common accommodations for mental health conditions include:
- Extended time on exams
- Reduced distraction testing environment
- Flexibility on attendance policies (with faculty agreement)
- Deadline extensions during documented flare-ups
- Note-taking support

## How to Apply

1. **Register with CAE:** Complete the online application at [cae.ucla.edu](https://www.cae.ucla.edu/) under "Apply for Services"
2. **Submit documentation:** A letter from your CAPS counselor, psychiatrist, or outside provider describing your diagnosis and functional limitations (not your full records)
3. **Meet with a CAE specialist:** Discuss your needs and what accommodations are appropriate
4. **Receive accommodation letters:** Share letters with professors each quarter

## Timeline

Start the process early. Documentation review can take 2–4 weeks. You cannot receive accommodations retroactively for assignments already submitted.

## Talking to Your Professor

CAE accommodation letters do not disclose your diagnosis. They state only that you are registered with CAE and entitled to specific accommodations. You are not required to explain your condition to your professor.
