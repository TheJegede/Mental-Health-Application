# Managing Finals Stress — Practical Guide

Finals season is one of the highest-stress periods of the academic year. Planning and support-seeking make a measurable difference.

## Extended CAPS Support During Finals

UCLA CAPS typically extends drop-in hours and adds additional Let's Talk slots during finals week. Check [counseling.ucla.edu](https://counseling.ucla.edu/) for the current finals schedule.

## Practical Stress Management

### Study strategy
- Distributed practice beats marathon sessions: 3 × 2-hour sessions outperform 1 × 6-hour session for retention
- Active recall (testing yourself) is more effective than re-reading
- Identify what you don't know early; study those areas first

### Sleep during finals
Sleep is not optional study time — it is when memory consolidation happens. Students who sleep 7–8 hours before an exam consistently outperform those who pull all-nighters, even on the same material.

### Eating
Skipping meals to study backfires. Blood sugar drops impair concentration and mood regulation. Keep snacks accessible and take actual meal breaks.

### Movement
Even 10 minutes of walking between study sessions improves focus and reduces cortisol.

## When Stress Crosses Into Crisis

Finals stress is normal. If you notice:
- Thoughts of not wanting to be here, or harming yourself
- Inability to function even with extended time
- Panic attacks that will not subside

Contact CAPS immediately at (310) 825-0768, or call/text 988. A bad finals week does not define your capability or worth — and there is support available right now.
