# Peer Counseling and Support at UCLA

Peer support programs are run by trained student volunteers who have personal experience navigating mental health challenges. They are not therapists, but they offer a low-barrier, relatable first point of contact.

## Bruin Peers

UCLA's peer counseling program trains student volunteers to:
- Have supportive conversations about mental health concerns
- Reduce stigma around seeking help
- Refer peers to appropriate campus resources

Drop-in hours are available in residential areas and student centers. No appointment needed.

## Resilience in Your Student Experience (RISE)

RISE is a resilience-building program offering:
- Workshops on stress, mindfulness, and academic pressure
- Peer-to-peer skills practice
- Facilitated group discussions

Check the CAPS website for current workshop schedule.

## How Peer Support Differs From Professional Counseling

| Peer support | Professional counseling |
|---|---|
| No waitlist | May have waitlist |
| Student volunteers | Licensed clinicians |
| Informal conversations | Structured therapy |
| Referral only, not treatment | Diagnosis and treatment |

Peer support is a good first step and a complement to professional services — not a replacement.

## If Your Peer Is in Crisis

If you are a student supporting a friend in crisis:
- Stay with them if safe to do so
- Call 988 together or encourage them to text HOME to 741741
- Call UCLA campus emergency: (310) 825-1491
- Contact CAPS directly: (310) 825-0768
