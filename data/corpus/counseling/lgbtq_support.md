# LGBTQ+ Mental Health Support at UCLA

LGBTQ+ students experience mental health challenges at higher rates than their peers — not because of their identity, but because of the stressors associated with stigma, discrimination, and navigating environments that are not always affirming.

## UCLA CAPS Affirming Services

- Counselors with experience and training in LGBTQ+ affirming care
- Group therapy for LGBTQ+ students offered most terms
- Affirming psychiatric services available
- All services fully confidential

## UCLA LGBTQ Campus Resource Center

The Campus Resource Center provides community, advocacy, and support specifically for LGBTQ+ students at UCLA:
- **Location:** Student Activities Center, A-Level
- **Website:** [lgbtq.ucla.edu](https://lgbtq.ucla.edu/)
- Drop-in hours, lounge space, referrals to campus and community resources

## Crisis Support for LGBTQ+ Youth

- **The Trevor Project:** 1-866-488-7386 | TrevorChat and TrevorText available at thetrevorproject.org
- **988 (Press 3):** After dialing 988, press 3 for the LGBTQ+ specialized crisis line operated by the Trevor Project
- **Trans Lifeline:** 877-565-8860 — staffed by trans people, for trans people in crisis

## Specific Concerns

- **Coming out:** Whether or not, when, and how to come out to family is a significant source of distress for many students. CAPS counselors can help you think through this.
- **Identity exploration:** You do not need to have your identity fully figured out to benefit from counseling.
- **Family rejection:** Research shows family acceptance is one of the strongest protective factors for LGBTQ+ youth wellbeing. If your family is not accepting, campus community resources and peer support are particularly important.
