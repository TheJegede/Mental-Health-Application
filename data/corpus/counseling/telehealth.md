# Telehealth Mental Health Services at UCLA

UCLA CAPS offers telehealth appointments for enrolled students who cannot attend in-person sessions or prefer remote access.

## What Telehealth Provides

- Individual therapy sessions via secure video platform
- Psychiatric consultations via video
- Same quality of care as in-person appointments

## How to Access

- Request a telehealth appointment when calling (310) 825-0768
- You will receive a secure link for your session
- Sessions require a private location where you can speak confidentially

## Technical Requirements

- Stable internet connection
- Device with camera and microphone (phone, laptop, or tablet)
- Private space — this is important; sessions cannot proceed if you cannot speak freely

## Important Limitations

Telehealth is not appropriate for:
- Acute crisis situations (call CAPS directly or 988)
- Students located outside California during sessions (licensing requirements)

## Additional Online Platforms Available to UCLA Students

- **WellTrack Boost:** CBT-based self-guided mental health app available to enrolled students at no cost — access through the MyUCLA portal or counseling.ucla.edu
- **Nod:** App focused on social anxiety and building connection skills

For crisis support, these apps are not appropriate. Use 988 or Crisis Text Line (HOME to 741741).
