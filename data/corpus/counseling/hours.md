# UCLA CAPS Hours and Locations

## Main Office

**Address:** John Wooden Center West, Suite A, UCLA Campus  
**Phone:** (310) 825-0768  
**Hours:** Monday–Friday, 8am–5pm

## After-Hours Support

A counselor is available **24 hours a day, 7 days a week** by calling (310) 825-0768 and selecting the after-hours option. This line is for students in crisis or acute distress.

## Satellite Locations

CAPS operates satellite offices in several residential and academic buildings across campus. Hours vary by location — call (310) 825-0768 or check [counseling.ucla.edu](https://counseling.ucla.edu/) for current satellite hours.

## Telehealth

UCLA CAPS offers telehealth appointments for enrolled students. Contact the main office to request a telehealth session.

## Crisis Resources (Always Available)

Even outside CAPS hours:
- Call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) — 24/7
- Text **HOME to 741741** (Crisis Text Line) — 24/7
- UCLA Police Welfare Check: (310) 825-1491
- Campus Emergency: 911 or (310) 825-1491
