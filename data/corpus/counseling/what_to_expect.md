# What to Expect at Your First CAPS Appointment

Many students feel nervous before their first counseling appointment. Here is what typically happens.

## Before the Appointment

You will complete a brief intake questionnaire online or in the waiting room. It asks about your reasons for seeking support, mental health history, and any current concerns. Your answers help the counselor understand your situation — answer honestly; there are no wrong answers.

## During the Intake Session

Your counselor will:
- Introduce themselves and explain confidentiality
- Ask open-ended questions about what brought you in
- Ask about your support system, academic situation, and any relevant history
- Discuss what kind of support might be most helpful

You do not need to have everything figured out. "I've been feeling off and didn't know where else to go" is a completely valid reason to come in.

## After the Intake

Based on the intake, your counselor will recommend one of the following:
- **Brief individual therapy** (typically 6–8 sessions at CAPS)
- **Group therapy** (often the most effective format and shorter waitlist)
- **Referral to community providers** for longer-term individual therapy
- **Psychiatric services** if medication evaluation is appropriate
- **Crisis services** if immediate safety is a concern

## Confidentiality

What you share with your counselor is confidential. Exceptions are limited to:
- Risk of harm to yourself or others
- Abuse or neglect of a minor, elder, or dependent adult
- Valid court order

Your counselor will explain this in detail before the session.

## It's Okay to Ask Questions

You can ask your counselor anything — about the process, your options, or how to make the most of the sessions. They are there to help, not to judge.
