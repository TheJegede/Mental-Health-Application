# Group Therapy at UCLA CAPS

Group therapy is one of the most effective forms of mental health treatment. UCLA CAPS offers a range of groups throughout the academic year.

## Why Group Therapy?

- Research consistently shows group therapy is as effective as individual therapy for many concerns
- Shorter waitlist than individual therapy
- Offers the unique benefit of connection with peers who share similar experiences
- Reduces feelings of isolation — knowing others struggle similarly is itself therapeutic

## Types of Groups Offered

Groups vary by term; the following types are typically offered:

| Group type | Focus |
|---|---|
| Anxiety support | Managing worry, social anxiety, and panic |
| Depression support | Mood, motivation, and self-compassion |
| Mindfulness-based | Stress reduction, present-moment awareness |
| Grief and loss | Processing loss of any kind |
| Relationship and communication | Patterns in relationships and communication |
| Identity and belonging | For students navigating identity, belonging, and cultural adjustment |
| International student support | Adjustment, cultural transitions, academic pressure |
| LGBTQ+ affirmative | Identity-affirming support for LGBTQ+ students |
| First-generation student support | Challenges specific to first-gen college experience |

## How to Join

Contact CAPS at (310) 825-0768 to inquire about current group offerings and availability. Groups typically meet weekly for 90 minutes.

## Confidentiality in Groups

Group members agree to keep what is shared in group confidential. Your counselor will review this expectation at the start of each group.
