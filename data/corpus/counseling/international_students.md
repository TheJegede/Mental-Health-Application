# Mental Health Resources for International Students

International students face a distinct set of stressors alongside all the standard pressures of college life.

## Common Challenges for International Students

- Cultural adjustment and homesickness
- Language and communication differences in academic settings
- Isolation from family support networks
- Visa status stress and immigration-related anxiety
- Academic pressure with higher perceived stakes ("I came here for this")
- Cultural backgrounds where mental health help-seeking is more stigmatized
- Financial pressures without access to US-based family support

## UCLA CAPS Services for International Students

- CAPS counselors are trained in cross-cultural competence
- Services are fully confidential — not reported to the university, visa sponsor, or home country
- Group therapy specifically for international students is typically offered
- Interpretation services available on request

## Your Immigration Status and Counseling

Seeking mental health support does not affect your visa status or academic standing. CAPS records are separate from all other university records.

## Bayit — International Student Community

UCLA hosts community spaces and events for international students facilitated by the Dashew Center for International Students and Scholars. These are peer connection opportunities, not clinical services.

Dashew Center: [internationalcenter.ucla.edu](https://internationalcenter.ucla.edu/)
