# Psychiatric Services at UCLA CAPS

UCLA CAPS offers psychiatric evaluations and medication management for enrolled students.

## What Psychiatric Services Involve

- **Psychiatric evaluation:** A 50-minute appointment with a CAPS psychiatrist or nurse practitioner to discuss symptoms, history, and whether medication might help
- **Medication management:** Follow-up appointments to monitor medication effectiveness and adjust dosage if needed

## Who Is This For?

Students whose symptoms may benefit from medication in addition to (or instead of) therapy. Common reasons include:
- Depression that has not improved with therapy alone
- Anxiety that significantly impacts daily functioning
- ADHD evaluation and medication
- Sleep conditions related to mental health

## How to Access Psychiatric Services

Psychiatric services are accessed through a referral from your CAPS therapist or through the intake process. Call (310) 825-0768 to inquire.

## Important Notes

- CAPS psychiatrists do not prescribe controlled substances for new patients without prior relationship; discuss this with your counselor
- For ongoing medication management, CAPS may refer you to an outside provider after initial evaluation
- Medication is one tool; it works best alongside other support

## Cost

Covered by student fees for enrolled students. Some specialized services may require a co-pay through Student Health insurance.
