# How to Schedule a Counseling Appointment at UCLA CAPS

UCLA Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS) offers a range of services for currently enrolled UCLA students. No prior mental health experience or diagnosis is needed to make an appointment.

## Step 1 — Initial Consultation

All new students begin with a **Let's Talk drop-in consultation** or a **phone triage appointment** to understand your concerns and match you with the right services.

- **Let's Talk:** Free, informal drop-in consultations with a counselor — no appointment needed
  - Multiple locations across campus
  - Limited to brief consultations; not ongoing therapy
- **Phone triage:** Call CAPS at (310) 825-0768 to speak with a counselor and schedule an intake

## Step 2 — Schedule an Intake Appointment

After initial contact, you will be scheduled for a full intake appointment. Intakes are typically 50 minutes.

- **Phone:** (310) 825-0768
- **Hours:** Monday–Friday, 8am–5pm
- **After hours and weekends:** A counselor on call is available 24/7 by calling the same number

## Same-Day Urgent Appointments

If you are in acute distress and need to be seen the same day, contact CAPS by phone when they open at 8am. Same-day urgent slots are limited.

## What to Bring

- Your BruinCard (UCLA student ID)
- A brief description of what you'd like to talk about — no need to have it figured out ahead of time

## Cost

Services are covered by student fees for enrolled UCLA students. No additional cost for most services.
